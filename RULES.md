# Serpentine Dream Chess — Rules

SERPENTINE DREAM CHESS
Chess Variant
by WhiteRabbitGeometry
(Benjamin Jason Ortlip)

SETUP:
Take a regular chess board.

The White Squares are Mirror Squares.
The Black Squares are Serpentine Squares.

RULES:
All pieces move and play as normal under regular circumstances.

The only change to normal chess rules is that Queens may not threaten each other.

SERPENTINE CHOICE:
NOTE: Pieces beginning the game on a Serpentine Square do not receive an initial Serpentine Choice. Until they move, they are their representational piece roles.

Non-King Pieces entering Serpentine Squares must choose another piece under their control. They now move, threaten, capture & act as the chosen piece, & maintain this identity until the piece enters a Mirror Square or is captured.

Upon entering a new Serpentine Square, the piece may exercise the option to make a new Serpentine Choice.

VICTORY & LOSS:
Unlike normal Chess, the object of Serpentine Dream Chess is to occupy & control the four central board squares.

LOSS:
Stalemate. Both players lose.

DRAW:
Checkmate.

VICTORY CONDITIONS:

CONVERGENCE:
A player finishes a turn with all four central squares occupied by pieces of their color.

ROYAL CONVERGENCE:
A player finishes a turn with both their King and Queen occupying the two central squares of their color.

OPTIONAL RULE:
Dream Mirror Bonus: At the start of their turn, Players who control (have piece protection on) both central squares of their opponent's color may rescue one previously captured pawn of their color by returning it to any unoccupied square on that player's starting pawn rank.
