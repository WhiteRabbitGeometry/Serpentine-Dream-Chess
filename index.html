Rules

Mirror / Serpentine

White squares are Mirror; black squares are Serpentine. A non-King entering Serpentine chooses a controlled active role; that role persists across Serpentine squares. Mirror restores representational identity.

Queens

Opposing representational Queens may not threaten or capture one another.

Victory

Convergence: occupy all four center squares. Royal Convergence: representational King and Queen occupy your two center-color squares. Checkmate is a draw. Stalemate means both players lose.

Dream Mirror Bonus

If, at the start of your turn, you protect both opponent-color center squares and have a captured pawn, you may rescue one to an open square on your starting pawn rank.

Computer

Random selects any legal action. Serpentine scores legal actions for challenge and mobility. Dream favors session-novel states. None can alter legality.
